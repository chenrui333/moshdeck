Privacy / updated September 16, 2026

Your terminal stays between your devices.

In the app

MoshDeck connects directly to the host you configure over SSH. There is no MoshDeck account, cloud relay, transcript analytics or remote prompt logging. The official Tailscale app is separate and operates under its own policies.

The SSH private key, saved profile and composer draft are stored in device-only Keychain storage. There is no private-key export or iCloud sync. The draft remains until you clear it. Public-key Copy and Share are intentional actions.

Device authentication protects access to the app. Inactive and locked terminal views are concealed. The app blocks remote OSC clipboard reads/writes and automatic URL opening. Clipboard content you explicitly copy is handled by iOS.

Diagnostics and beta feedback

Local connection diagnostics contain attempt identifiers, timings, stages and safe error categories, and may include public fingerprints or host/profile information. They are designed not to contain terminal text, pasted prompts, passwords or private keys. You choose whether to share them.

Apple TestFlight may collect and share crash information, usage information and feedback according to Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. Review screenshots and diagnostics before sending them.

This website

This is a static informational site. It has no signup form, analytics script, cookies or browser storage added by MoshDeck. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. Following external links is subject to those sites’ policies.
